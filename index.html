Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar, government subsidies, and elevated terrace structures.

Will installing solar panels block or ruin my terrace space?

Not at all! Unlike traditional low-mounted frames that make roofs unusable, Shourya Infra specializes in custom **Elevated Terrace Sheds** (typically 7 to 9 feet tall). You can walk underneath, dry clothes, host rooftop barbecues, and enjoy cooling shade that protects your ceiling from harsh summer heat.

How much subsidy will I receive under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, individual residential houses receive:
  • 1 kW: ₹30,000 subsidy
  • 2 kW: ₹60,000 subsidy
  • 3 kW and above: Flat ₹78,000 subsidy
The subsidy is deposited directly into your linked bank account after DISCOM net-meter inspection. Shourya Infra assists you with the entire paperless application!

What are Bifacial solar panels and why are they better?

Bifacial panels have transparent glass on both sides. In addition to absorbing sunlight directly from above, they absorb light reflected from your white terrace floor onto the back side. This yields up to 15% to 25% more units of electricity per day compared to older monofacial panels.

What happens during rainy or cloudy days?

Solar panels generate power from ambient daylight (photons), so they continue producing electricity even on overcast days. Furthermore, with Telangana's On-Grid Net Metering, excess power generated during sunny days is credited back to your DISCOM meter, which you can use freely at night or during rainy weeks!

Is solar safe during thunderstorms and high winds?

Yes, 100%! Shourya Infra uses heavy-gauge Hot-Dip Galvanized structures engineered to withstand winds up to 150 km/h. Every installation includes a dedicated **Copper Lightning Arrestor** and multi-stage **Chemical Earthing Pits** with surge protection devices (SPDs) to shield your equipment and house against lightning strikes.
